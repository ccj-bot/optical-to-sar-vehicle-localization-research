**1. Data Hierarchy**

- **L0:** Complete three-scene temporal streams. Each scene is about 15 seconds and includes optical frames, SAR gray frames, SAR pseudo-color frames, DepthPro outputs, track context, and candidate context.
- **L1:** All full-stream transfer opportunities: optical detections, tracks, SAR candidates, correspondence windows, temporal windows, and continuity cues.
- **L2:** The **231 GT-reviewed car samples**. These are supervised evaluation and diagnostic windows, not the full dataset.
- **L3:** Stage 1 / Stage 2 / Stage 3 controlled subsets inside the 231 samples.
- **L4:** Individual diagnostic samples such as `gm_rm019_00006` and `gm_rm017_00080`.

**2. Why the 231 Samples Are Only an Evaluation Subset**

The full research object is the complete three-scene stream, not the 231 samples. The 231 are only the subset with GT or manually reviewed SAR boxes, used for supervised evaluation, diagnosis, controlled experiments, failure analysis, and validating logic. They are a measurement window into the larger L0/L1 transfer problem.

**3. What Stage 1/2/3 Mean**

- **Stage 1:** Reliable or widened old range-anchor samples. Currently used as a debugging gate.
- **Stage 2:** Bottom-truncated or near-field range-shift samples. Must not run until Stage 1 passes.
- **Stage 3:** Multi-range compact hypothesis samples. Must not run until Stage 1 and Stage 2 are understood.

These are L3 diagnostic subsets, not the main research objective.
