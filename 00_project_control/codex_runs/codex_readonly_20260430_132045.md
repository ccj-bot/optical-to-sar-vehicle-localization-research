**1. Data Hierarchy**
- **L0:** Complete three-scene temporal streams, roughly 15 seconds each, including optical frames, SAR gray frames, SAR pseudo-color frames, DepthPro outputs, and available track/candidate context.
- **L1:** Full-stream transfer opportunities: optical detections/tracks, SAR candidates, correspondence opportunities, temporal windows, and track continuity cues.
- **L2:** The 231 GT-reviewed car samples. These are supervised evaluation and diagnostic windows.
- **L3:** Stage 1/2/3 controlled subsets inside the 231 samples.
- **L4:** Individual diagnostic samples such as `gm_rm019_00006` or `gm_rm017_00080`.

**2. Why The 231 Samples Are Only An Evaluation Subset**
The full research object is the complete three-scene temporal stream, not the 231 reviewed samples. The 231 are only the subset with GT or manually reviewed SAR boxes, used for supervised evaluation, quantitative diagnosis, controlled experiments, failure analysis, and validating or rejecting logic. They are a measurement window into the larger L0/L1 transfer problem.
